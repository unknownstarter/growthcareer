International students can work part-time, but working with no preparation is illegal. If you go through the required permit process, you can work legally.

## The Default Rule Is No Profit-Making Activity

Foreigners staying on a study status are, in principle, barred from profit-making activity. You can work only if you have received a part-time work permit (permission for activities outside your status) from the competent immigration office in advance.

## Where to Apply

International students (D-2) and language trainees (D-4-1) can apply for the part-time work permit online at [Government24](https://www.gov.kr). The application requires a part-time work recommendation letter that your school has reviewed and signed.

If you run into trouble with the application or online processing does not work, you can contact the Immigration Contact Center (1345). If an in-person submission is needed, you book a visit through HiKorea.

## Processing Time and Fee

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Processing time | Within 14 days (usually about 3 days) |
| Fee | Waived |
| How to apply | Online at Government24, or book a HiKorea visit |

## Working Before the Permit Is Unauthorized

Part-time work is valid only after immigration approval is complete. Working ahead of time while the review is in progress counts as unauthorized work, and unauthorized work can lead to penalties for violating the Immigration Act.

> What penalties apply to unauthorized work depends on the situation, so this article does not assert them. For specifics, confirm with the Immigration Contact Center (1345).

## Workplace and Duration

The permit lasts up to one year at a time, and you are limited to two workplaces. If you change your workplace, you must file a change report.

## Always Check Allowed Hours in the Latest Source

Allowed hours differ during the semester and during vacation, and there can be differences by level. Specific figures such as weekly allowed hours change often through policy revisions, so this article does not list them. Always confirm the hours granted with your permit in the [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr) manual or at the Immigration Contact Center (1345).
