D-2 (study) is one of Korea's residence statuses. For accurate visa information, checking the official channels is the safest approach.

## Official Guidance Is on HiKorea

For official guidance on D-2 study visa residence status, check HiKorea. HiKorea is the channel run by the Korea Immigration Service under the Ministry of Justice.

It provides official information on residence, extensions, and changes.

## Where to Confirm Detailed Requirements

You can confirm detailed requirements in HiKorea's status-by-status guidance manual or at the Immigration Contact Center (1345).
